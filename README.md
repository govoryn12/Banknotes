# Cash Counter (Купюрник)

A single-file, offline cash counter. Enter how many banknotes and coins you have, and it shows per-denomination amounts, subtotals and the grand total.

- **Currencies:** Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH), US dollar (USD), euro (EUR)
- **Banknotes and coins:** coins can be switched on or off (off by default)
- **Export:** save as PDF (via print) or as a CSV spreadsheet
- **Languages:** Ukrainian and English
- **Saved locally:** quantities, currency and language are remembered in your browser

No installation or dependencies: just open `kupiurnyk.html` in a browser.
